# CTP Magazine — Project brief for Muse

Date: 2026-11-19. Source: Resofactor, verbatim brief given in chat.

## What it is

ctp-magazine.github.io, a curated news front door for the CTP audience. 114 verified and cleared RSS feeds, 271 articles after de-duplication, six topic filters with Consciousness and Near-Death Experiences split out, masthead, square badge and photo strip, takedowns routed to takedown@ctp-energy.com and syndication to syndication@ctp-energy.com.

## The standard to hold

- Every card short paragraphs, no wall of excerpt.
- No duplicates, ever.
- Images lowercase and stripped of metadata, each one opening its source page.
- Each topic returns its own stories rather than the whole pool.

## Open items, in order

1. **Cap arXiv at three or four cards on the newest list** and let the other feeds compete, because 125 of 271 from one source turns the front page into a pre-print dump.
2. **Upload the twelve-hour auto-update workflow with Alfred in one sitting, via GitHub's web editor**, since that is the same job as the ISO build and needs no Linux machine.
3. **Then FINDSP's site.**

## Rules

- Never state a first-party claim the source does not make.
- Credit and link the publisher on every card.
- Nothing from a feed that has not been verified and cleared.
- A takedown comes down first and gets argued afterwards.

## Reporting

Report what shipped and what is still open, in plain counts.
